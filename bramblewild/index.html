Find your footing

WASD / arrows
Move relative to the camera.
Q / E
Hold to turn your character left / right. Keep that facing while moving; mouse aiming, selecting a destination or X returns to automatic facing. The on-screen Turn buttons work the same way.
Ctrl / Sprint
Hold before clicking a destination, or while moving, for a faster burst. Sprint spends stamina; guard and attacks take priority. After exhaustion, release to catch your breath before sprinting again. V is an alternate key if your browser captures Ctrl shortcuts.
F / Cut button
Tap for a quick cut; hold up to about two-thirds of a second for more power, then release. Stronger swings spend more stamina and recover more slowly. Left/right relative to your facing chooses the sweep; forward steps into it, backward withdraws. Direction is fixed when you release.
R / Thrust button
Thrust with a sword or spear; heavy cut with an axe.
C / Bash button
A quick, close shield strike. Interrupts an unguarded foe and pushes them back. Costs stamina and needs an equipped shield.
Hold Shift / RT
Guard the front. Raise your shield just before contact to parry. Preparing a cut or using melee drops your guard, including while moving; held guard returns after recovery.
Space
Step in your movement direction, or backward while still.
G / H
Interact / drink a healing draught. J and K also remain available for cut and thrust.
I or B / M
Equipment / field map. Both pause the solo expedition.
Mouse and touch
Click or tap ground to move; an enemy to approach and repeat basic cuts. Movement, guard and special attacks take over immediately. Choose ground, press X or use Release to disengage. Drag to orbit. Disable Tap combat assist in Settings for manual attacks.
Gamepad L3 / LT / R3
Press left stick click to step in your movement direction, or backward while still. Hold the left trigger to sprint, including before moving; release to return to normal movement. Press right stick click to select the nearest visible enemy when unlocked, or cycle targets when already locked. D-pad right releases the target; then R3 selects the nearest again. Controller actions can be remapped in Settings → Controller.
Camera / pause
Drag to orbit, wheel to zoom. Esc opens the expedition journal. Maps, equipment and settings pause solo play.

Enemies commit to their strikes. Step outside the swing and answer during recovery. A fresh guard can parry; an exhausted guard can break.
