# Inkdrift

Pilot a small cartoon ship through procedural asteroid fields and land on illustrated worlds.

[Play Inkdrift](https://actiondaveinri.github.io/projects/inkdrift/) · [Archive](https://actiondaveinri.github.io/projects/inkdrift/archive/) · [All projects](https://actiondaveinri.github.io/)

`index.html` is the current standalone Inkdrift tech demo. **Inkstar is its early generation**, preserved under `archive/inkstar/` with its original builds and screenshot. Old Inkstar launch URLs still lead to the corresponding archived builds.
