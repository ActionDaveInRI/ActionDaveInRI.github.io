View & controls

How to explore

Tap a star or world to mark it. Choose Travel, or double-tap the marked target to visit.

Drag to orbit. Pinch or scroll to zoom. Tap terrain to inspect it.

World-list and map buttons visit directly. Scene taps only mark a target until you choose Travel. Use View relief to see a sunlit landform at an angle.

Esc closes a panel, then goes up a level. ＋ / − zoom. Focus the scene and use arrow keys to orbit. H hides controls.

Generation & graphics

Generation settings rebuild the cluster. The same seed reproduces the same layout.

Scene

Tap a star to preview it, then choose Travel. Press Esc to close this menu.

Stars

Nebula

Seeded filaments, hollow shells and cloud knots form stable landmarks. Their glow softens as you approach a world.

System

System contents are generated deterministically per-star (seed + star index).
