CONTROLLER

Click inside the game, then press a controller button.

Tap the D-pad and squeeze both triggers, then release everything before starting. Setup asks for each button once.

The readout responds while paused. If an unrecognized controller uses the usual Xbox / PlayStation layout, try standard button order. A / ✕ or B / ○ resumes; Menu pauses. Open game in its own tab.

SHIP AUDIO
Two engines. One familiar rattle.

On foot, right-click or tap the floor to walk there. G sits on a clear bunk, bench or chair. Space climbs a low clear obstacle or lowers you back down. Move to stand up. Hands need a holstered weapon. In flight, guns follow the ship’s nose. Cruise coasts when thrust is released. Port guidance takes over as you approach; C lets the copilot fly the whole route. At port, R / V changes height; Space brakes all drift. Match the berth heading, center over the pad, and descend below 0.65 m/s. C engages assistance. Relay requires passage through the open bay doors before capture. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Touch: the right stick aims quietly. Hold and drag FIRE to shoot and aim with one thumb while moving with the other.
